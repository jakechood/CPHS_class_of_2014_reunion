Information

Como‐Pickton High School's Class of 2014 invites you to celebrate the decinnial of our graduation! Please vote or RSVP below to let us know which event(s) you can make it to! Our current plan is to informally attend Homecoming at CPHS in Como as a group on Friday, 20th September 2024 at 7PM, followed by a main reunion gathering at Roma's Italian Bar and Grill in Sulphur Springs on 9th November, 2024. Please let us know in the RSVP section below which event(s) you are able to join us for! If you choose to reserve a ticket(s), please ensure to complete a CheddarUp reservation to complete your RSVP.

*If anyone would like to donate more than their tickets' worth toward the reunion fund, the remainder will go toward others' cover charges and/or a higher tip for the restaurant staff.*

Events

Homecoming
  • Como, Texas
  • 20th Sep 2024, 6:30PM ‐ 9PM

If you're in town on 20th September 2024, join us at the homecoming game! See our alma mater's upgraded facilities, current sports and performance teams, and enjoy nostalgic concessions with us while we support our educational descendants!

Reunion
  • Roma's Italian Bar and Grill
  • 400 Shannon Rd E, Sulphur Springs, Texas 75482
  • 9th November, 2024

At our main event, join us for cameraderie, food, and adult beverages as we celebrate our journeys together! Don't forget to bring your yearbooks and markers to collect updated signatures.
