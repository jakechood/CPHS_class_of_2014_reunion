Como‐Pickton High School's Class of 2014 invites you to celebrate the decinnial of our graduation!

Events

Homecoming
  • Como, Texas
  • 20 Sep 2024, 6:30PM ‐ 9PM

If you're in town the night before the reunion, join us at the homecoming game! See our alma mater's upgraded facilities, current sports and performance teams, and enjoy nostalgic concessions with us while we support our educational descendants!

Reunion
  • Roma's Italian Bar and Grill
  • 400 Shannon Rd E, Sulphur Springs, Texas 75482
  • 21 Sep 2024, 3:30PM ‐ 5:30PM

At our main event, join us for cameraderie, food, and adult beverages as we celebrate our journeys together! Don't forget to bring your yearbooks and markers to collect updated signatures.
